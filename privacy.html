Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

What We Collect

When you use Will AI Replace Me?, we may collect the following information:

  • Quiz responses — your job title, quiz answers, and resulting score. These are processed in your browser and are not stored on our servers.
  • Email address — only if you voluntarily provide it to receive your results. We do not sell or share your email with third parties.
  • Payment information — if you purchase the deep analysis, payment is processed securely through Stripe. We never see or store your credit card number.
  • Deep analysis conversations — if you purchase a deep analysis, your conversation is processed by Anthropic's Claude API and is not stored after your session ends.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with your career resilience score and, if purchased, your personalized deep analysis. We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or build profiles about you.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe — for secure payment processing. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to power the AI deep analysis conversation. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Netlify — for website hosting. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — to understand how visitors use our site. This collects anonymous usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies required for site functionality and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention

Quiz results are processed entirely in your browser and are not stored on our servers. Email addresses are retained only for the purpose of delivering results. Payment records are maintained by Stripe per their retention policies.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any personal data we hold (such as your email address) by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at the email listed on our website.
